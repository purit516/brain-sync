Privacy Policy — brain-sync

Last updated: 2026-10-21

brain-sync is a personal application operated by and for a single individual (the "Owner"). It accesses the Owner's own Google Drive, limited to a single folder designated by the Owner, in order to synchronize the Owner's personal notes between Google Drive and a computer the Owner controls.

Data accessed

File contents and metadata of the designated Drive folder only, via the Google Drive API, under an OAuth client the Owner created in the Owner's own Google Cloud project.

Data use

Data is used solely to perform synchronization for the Owner. It is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party; not used for advertising; and not used to train models.

Data storage and retention

Synchronized copies reside on the Owner's own devices and the Owner's assistant environment. The Owner may revoke access at any time from the Owner's Google Account permissions page, which immediately stops all access.

Contact

The Owner can be reached at the support email listed on the application's consent screen.
